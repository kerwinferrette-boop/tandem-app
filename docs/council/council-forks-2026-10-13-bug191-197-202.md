## What I verified myself, and what it changed

**Two findings no advisor had, from the live database:**

1. **`users.color_theme` DOES NOT EXIST in Postgres.** `information_schema.columns` for
   `public.users` returns only `theme_color` (plus `avatar_hair_color`). So `tandem.html:11083`,
   `sb.from('users').upsert({ id, color_theme: hex })`, names a column that is not there and
   **fails on every colour pick**. It is not awaited and has no `.catch()`; supabase-js resolves
   with `{ error }` rather than throwing, so the failure is swallowed in total silence — no
   console warning, no toast, nothing. This reframes the fork: there is no dead column to drop,
   only a broken write to delete, and *no migration is needed*. It also corrects my own
   `scripts/column-reachability-smoke.mjs`, whose `KNOWN_OPEN` note called it "written, read by
   nothing" — true of the client, but it understates the defect. That gate is static and
   client-only, which its header says, and this is the limitation biting.
2. **`week_targets` is almost entirely the column default.** 81 `personal_records` rows: 81
   non-null, but **70 are the `'{}'` default** from `migrations/calibration_v05.sql:8` and only
   **11 carry real computed data**. Retirement discards 11 rows of unused cache.

**Two advisor claims I overturned, and did not relay:**

- The Expansionist asserted `workout_sessions` "stores **no day identity at all** (grep: no
  `day_key` column anywhere)". Literally true of the name `day_key` and **false in substance**:
  `day_type` holds exactly that, written at `:5812`, `:6131`, `:7286`, `:7449` and read at
  `:8214` and in `syncFromCloud`'s `lastDone.day_type`. So "the schema cannot support an order"
  is NOT a valid argument against BUG-191, and the verdict does not rest on it.
- Three advisors treated the day tabs as fully serving the swap need. Verified half-true:
  `tab.onclick = () => switchDay(day.key, tab)` (`:5350`) and `switchDay()` (`:7637`) do swap the
  visible workout — but **within the session only**. `switchDay` sets the global `currentDay` and
  toggles `.workout-view`; it never persists, and `renderTracker` re-derives `currentDay` from the
  queue on every render (`:5376`), writing `tandem_current_day` as a "reconciled cache, not the
  source of truth" (`:5379`). So a tab swap survives until the next render, not until tomorrow.

**Three advisor claims I confirmed, which carry the BUG-202 verdict:**

- `getWeekTarget()` (`:3158`) → `weekFactor()` (`:3130`) computes the per-week target **live, in
  the real prescription path**, at `:3414`, `:5528` and `:5542`. `computeWeekTargets()` (`:3187`)
  runs the same `weekFactor` loop and freezes the result. So `week_targets` is a **stale cache of
  a pure function** whose inputs (`cfg.goal`, `cfg.weeks`, the 1RM) all move underneath it.
  Wiring it in would *create* the second home, not close one. That inverts the fork as filed.
- `scripts/calibration-upsert-smoke.mjs:124-125` **already** asserts the stored-best invariant —
  `best_estimated_1rm_lbs === 400` for the higher historical PR — on a column that is genuinely
  read. `:126-127` asserts the same fact a second time *through the dead value*. Dropping `:119`
  and `:126-127` while keeping `:124-125` loses no coverage.
- `tandem_day_order` has exactly three references: the scoped-key list (`:2807`) and the read and
  write inside `reorderWeek()` itself (`:7308`, `:7313`). `nextProgramDayKey()` (`:9843`) is four
  lines and consults none of them.

## Where the council agreed, beyond the verdicts

- **"Honor the order" was never a live option.** It would make `tandem_day_order` a second home
  for queue position, competing with `completedSessionCount() % perWeek` — the exact silo the
  project's rule forbids — and bounding it would require inventing a number ("max two pushbacks")
  no source supplies. It also reopens Kerwin's BUG-93 ruling that *"a missed workout is DELAYED,
  not skipped"*, which is his to reopen, not an agent's.
- **The toast is the real defect, not the no-op.** `showToast('Moved to the back of this week —
  nothing lost')` manufactures confidence, which is strictly worse than a button that visibly does
  nothing. And its promise is already the queue's default behaviour, so there is nothing to wire.
- **BUG-197 is two rules, not four homes.** Rule A, identity colour — "which colour am I in the
  competition" — has one healthy home, `users.theme_color`, read by both partners. Rule B, the
  live app accent, currently belongs to `applyColorTakeover()` by design. The picker is a second,
  contradicting owner of **Rule B**, not a fourth home of Rule A. Only Rule A's plumbing is
  shippable; Rule B is aesthetic.
- **Wiring `week_targets` would be worse than leaving it dead.** Unanimous, and the Expansionist —
  whose job is finding upside — said plainly there is none here.

## Where it clashed

**Only on BUG-197's design half, and the council deliberately did not resolve it** (correctly —
`design_changes_hold` makes it Kerwin's):

- The Outsider: the takeover is right on the dashboard and *wrong* as a global accent — "a theme
  that flips when I navigate feels broken no matter how intentional it is."
- The Expansionist: the takeover is "the most distinctive mechanic in Tandem... an ambient
  scoreboard you feel before you read it", and if anything should be scoped it is the picker, not
  the takeover — scope the takeover **up**, app-wide.
- The Contrarian found the concrete defect under that disagreement: `applyAccentColor()` has
  exactly three callers (`:10081` takeover, `:11074` picker, `:11230` boot) and **nothing ever
  restores your colour when you leave the dashboard**, while `applyAccentColor` also overwrites
  `--green`/`--teal`. So today, if Dani is ahead, Kerwin's whole app — tracker, settings,
  everything — stays her colour for the rest of the session.
